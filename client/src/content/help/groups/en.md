# Do you need a group?

**If you follow swimmers on your own, you don’t need a group — use Favorites.** Favorites is your personal list: only you see it. Mark your own kids with the gold heart — that’s your Family, with its own page, [My favorites](route:myFavorites). Favorites hold up to 30 swimmers and 3 clubs.

**A group is for several people who share something:**

- one list of swimmers that every member sees, not each on their own;
- trainings, a schedule, “going / not going”, lanes;
- shared photos and videos;
- members and join requests.

When you **don’t** need a group: a parent creating “My kids and their friends”. That’s Favorites — nobody but that parent would do anything in such a group.

## When a group makes sense

**A training camp across clubs.** Parents whose kids go to the same camp create “Eilat camp 2026”: they add the kids from the results, share videos and follow their races. Several people, one list, shared media — that’s a group.

**A coach’s group.** A coach runs a masters squad: the schedule, who’s coming to practice, lanes by level, training times. Some swimmers are in the federation results, some aren’t (the coach adds those — see below).

**Your club’s official group.** The club gets its own place on the site: the roster comes from the federation results automatically, and the group’s photos and videos can be shown to everyone. How to become official — below.

**A closed circle.** If outsiders shouldn’t see the group, untick **Public group** and let people in by request. It won’t appear in the catalog; only members see the page and its media.

## What “Following” means

A group’s list is called **Following** — the group follows these swimmers. You can add any swimmer from the federation results, and the group doesn’t claim they swim for it. That’s why coach and captain roles are visible only to whoever runs the group; everyone else sees plain members. A club’s list is called **Swimmers** — it comes from federation data.

## People without an account or a loglig profile

If someone trains with the group but isn’t in the federation results and doesn’t want an account, the coach adds them: **+ Add a swimmer who isn’t on loglig**. Such a swimmer is visible **only inside the group** — no public page, not in search. You can put them in lanes and record their training times.

Stopped coming? **Deactivate** removes them from the list and lanes and keeps their times; **Restore** brings them back. To delete them for good (times included), use the Inactive list.

## Open or by request

- **Open** — anyone with an account joins right away **and immediately sees members-only media**.
- **By request** — people send a request and you approve it; until then they don’t see members-only media.

Sharing photos and videos of kids? Choose By request. Change it in the **Joining** card on the group’s **Admin** tab (or when you create the group). A group that isn’t public is always join-by-request, whatever the switch says.

## Who sees photos and videos

- **Members** — group members only. Every group has it.
- **Everyone 🌐** — every visitor: photos and videos show in results and on swimmer pages. Only **Trusted** groups have it. The site admin marks a group Trusted — just ask. Your club’s official group is trusted automatically.

Why: a group can add any swimmer, so anything that reaches someone else’s page has to come from a source the site trusts. Media reported by several people is hidden everywhere until the admin decides.

## How many groups you can create

Anyone who’s signed in can create groups and own up to **3** (official ones count too). Need more? Ask the site admin to raise your limit. You can be an admin in any number of other people’s groups.

## Your club’s official group

1. Create a group.
2. In **My groups** → **Edit** → **Official club status**, pick your club and submit the request.
3. The site admin reviews and approves it.

Once approved, the group follows its club — the roster arrives from the results by itself — and becomes Trusted. Other groups with the same name get “ · community” added, and groups that followed the same club leave the catalog (their links keep working).

**Search before you create.** If your club already has an official group, join it — a second copy won’t appear in the catalog. [Browse groups](route:groupsList)
