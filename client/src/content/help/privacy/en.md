# Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026.

SwimHub (swimhub.co.il) shows Israeli swimming competition results and records. It is run by a private individual in Israel. Questions, corrections and removal requests: **privacy@swimhub.co.il**.

## What we show about swimmers

Everything on swimmer, club and competition pages comes from **published sources**: official results of the Israel Swimming Association and the competition protocols it publishes (loglig.com), and record lists of World Aquatics and national federations. For a swimmer that means:

- name (in Hebrew and, where available, in English), birth year, club;
- races: times, places, medals, points, split times — as printed in the protocol.

From these results we calculate our own figures: personal and season bests, levels, places among swimmers of the same age, head-to-head comparisons.

We show what the protocol says. If a protocol has an obvious error, we keep the official figures and mark the line “Under review” — we don’t silently change official data.

## Accounts

You don’t need an account to look at results. An account lets you keep favorites, join groups and add videos. For an account we store:

- your email, display name and, if you sign in with Google, your Google profile picture;
- a password hash (never the password itself), if you use email and password;
- sign-in history — date, time and IP address — to protect your account. It is deleted after about 90 days;
- what you add yourself: favorites, groups, links to photos and videos, reactions.

Sign-in uses one cookie that keeps you signed in. Display settings (light or dark mode, the language of explanations) are kept in your browser. We don’t use advertising or tracking cookies, and we don’t sell or share your data for marketing.

## Children

- **You need to be 13 or older to create an account.** When you sign up you confirm it; if you are under 18, you also confirm that a parent or guardian knows you use SwimHub. We don’t ask for documents.
- Children under 13 don’t get their own account. A parent can follow their child from the parent’s account — mark the child with the gold heart in Favorites.
- A coach who adds swimmers to a group — including swimmers who aren’t in the federation results, their training times and videos — confirms that the swimmers’ parents agreed to it. Swimmers added this way are visible only inside the group.

## Search engines

Competition, club and swimmer pages can appear in search engines such as Google. Groups, your favorites and your media never do.

**If you want a swimmer’s page out of search engines**, write to privacy@swimhub.co.il. We will tell search engines not to index that page and take it out of our sitemap. The page itself stays reachable by its link, and the results stay part of the competition, because they are official public results.

## Your rights

Under the Israeli Privacy Protection Law you may ask to see the information we hold about you and to correct it. You can also ask us to delete your account and everything you added. Write to **privacy@swimhub.co.il** — we answer within 30 days.

If the data about a swimmer is wrong, tell us. If the mistake is in the official protocol, we will mark it and report it to the source; the protocol itself can only be corrected by the federation.

## Where the data is kept

- the site and its database — Microsoft Azure, Israel Central region;
- emails (account confirmation, password reset) are sent through Resend;
- “Sign in with Google” is handled by Google — we receive only your name, email and profile picture.

## Changes

If we change this policy, the new version appears on this page with a new date. A significant change that affects account holders — we will also announce it on the site.
