# Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026.

SwimHub (swimhub.co.il) shows results of swimming competitions in Israel and swimming records. This policy explains what information we hold, why, who receives it and what you can ask of us. Using the site is also governed by the [Terms of use](route:terms).

## Who we are

SwimHub is run by a private individual in Israel, Vladimir Barentsev, who is the controller of the site’s database. Contact for anything about your data: **privacy@swimhub.co.il**. General questions: info@swimhub.co.il.

## Information about swimmers

**Where it comes from.** Everything on competition, swimmer and club pages comes from published sources: the official results of the Israel Swimming Association and the competition protocols it publishes on loglig.com, and the record lists of World Aquatics and national federations. We do not ask swimmers for this information.

**What it is.** A swimmer’s name (in Hebrew and, where available, in English), birth year, club and races — times, places, medals, points and split times, as printed in the protocol. From these results we calculate our own figures: personal and season bests, levels, places among swimmers of the same age, head-to-head comparisons.

**Why.** To show competition results and a swimmer’s progress in one place — the purpose for which the federation publishes them.

**Accuracy.** We show what the protocol says. If a protocol has an obvious error, the official figures stay and the line is marked “Under review”; we don’t change official data quietly. The protocol itself can only be corrected by the federation — we report errors to it.

## Information about account holders

You don’t need an account to look at results. **Giving us information for an account is voluntary — there is no legal obligation to do so.** Without it you can’t keep favorites, join groups or add videos; everything else works.

What we keep and why:

- **email, display name** and, if you sign in with Google, your **Google profile picture** — to run your account and send account emails (confirming your address, resetting your password);
- a **password hash**, never the password itself, if you use email and password — to let you sign in;
- the **confirmation that you are 13 or older** and when you gave it — see “Children”;
- **sign-in history** — date, time and IP address — to protect your account from misuse;
- **what you add yourself**: favorites, groups and their members, trainings, links to photos and videos, reactions — to show them to you and to whoever you share them with.

We don’t use your information for advertising, we don’t sell it and we don’t pass it to anyone for marketing. We don’t make automated decisions about you.

## Who receives information

Only service providers that run the site for us, each for its own task:

- **Microsoft Azure** (Israel Central region) — hosting of the site and its database;
- **Resend** — sending account emails;
- **Google** — “Sign in with Google”; we receive only your name, email and profile picture;
- **Cloudflare** — the site’s domain name and email routing for swimhub.co.il.

We disclose information to authorities only when the law requires it.

## Children

- **You need to be 13 or older to create an account.** When you sign up you confirm it, and if you are under 18 you also confirm that a parent or guardian knows you use SwimHub. We don’t ask for documents.
- Children under 13 don’t get their own account. A parent can follow their child from the parent’s own account — mark the child with the gold heart in Favorites.
- A coach who adds swimmers to a group — including swimmers who aren’t in the federation results, their training times and videos — confirms that the swimmers’ parents agreed to it. Swimmers added this way are visible only inside the group.

## Search engines

Competition, club and swimmer pages can appear in search engines such as Google. Groups, your favorites and your media never do.

**If you want a swimmer’s page out of search engines**, write to privacy@swimhub.co.il. We will tell search engines not to index that page and remove it from our sitemap. The page stays reachable by its link and the results stay part of the competition, because they are official public results.

## How long we keep information

- swimmers’ results — as long as the site exists, as part of the competition record;
- account information and what you added — until you delete it or ask us to delete the account;
- sign-in history — about 90 days;
- backups of the database — 14 days.

## Your rights

Under the Israeli Protection of Privacy Law you may ask to see the information we hold about you and ask us to correct or delete information that is wrong, incomplete or out of date. You can also ask us to delete your account and everything you added. A parent may make these requests for a child. Write to **privacy@swimhub.co.il** — we answer within 30 days. If you think we got it wrong, you may complain to the Privacy Protection Authority (gov.il).

## Cookies and your browser

Signing in uses a cookie that keeps you signed in, and a security cookie that protects forms. Display settings — light or dark mode, the language of explanations — are kept in your browser. We don’t use advertising or tracking cookies and no third-party analytics.

## Security

Connections to the site are encrypted, passwords are stored only as hashes, access to the database is limited to the operator. No system is perfectly safe; if a breach puts your information at risk, we will tell you and the authority as the law requires.

## Changes

If we change this policy, the new version appears on this page with a new date. A significant change that affects account holders we will also announce on the site.
