# What joining a group means

**A group is a team that trains together.** Join if you swim with this group, or your child does.

After you join:

- you see the group’s trainings, schedule and lanes;
- before each training you are asked “going / not going”, and the coach and the other members see who is coming;
- you see the photos and videos shared inside the group;
- the coach sees your name and email in the list of members.

**Only want to follow these swimmers’ results?** You don’t need to join — add them to [Favorites](route:myFavorites).

---

You can leave at any time: ✓ Member → Leave group.
